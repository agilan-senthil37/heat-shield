# Environmental data shortlist

Reviewed 2026-11-13. Implementation now uses MRLC 2021 canopy/land-cover WCS, Landsat via Planetary Computer, CPAD holdings and FEMA footprints; see supabase/README.md and environment_sources.json. This table records the original source-selection research. National products cover all nine Bay Area counties plus Tracy and Mountain House. Confirm the selected asset's acquisition dates, CRS, nodata, resolution and terms before loading; store those with the resulting indicators.

| Input | Provider and source | Access | Use and limitations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Surface temperature | USGS [Landsat Collection 2 Surface Temperature](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2-surface-temperature) | Level-2 scene downloads through EarthExplorer; select scenes covering the study region and a fixed summer window | Satellite-derived land-surface temperature, not measured air temperature. Apply cloud/shadow/fill QA masks; preserve acquisition dates and valid-pixel fraction. DN × 0.00341802 + 149 gives kelvin; subtract 273.15 for Celsius. Do not populate `heat_observed_c`; add a separately named surface-temperature estimate field. |
| Tree canopy | USDA Forest Service [Tree Canopy Cover](https://data.fs.usda.gov/geodata/rastergateway/treecanopycover/) | Annual CONUS raster downloads and standard-error products; MRLC also distributes NLCD TCC | 30 m modeled canopy, not an inventory of individual trees. Science product includes standard error and may predict canopy over water/cropland. Postprocessed NLCD TCC applies masks. Pin a year/product version and keep its uncertainty metadata. |
| Land cover and impervious surface | USGS [Annual NLCD access](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/eros/science/annual-nlcd-data-access) | MRLC area-of-interest GeoTIFF download, ScienceBase downloads, web services; AWS path documented but requester-pays | Exclude mapped open water; conservatively flag wetlands and developed impervious land. Impervious percentage cannot identify individual sidewalks, buildings, or planting strips at 30 m. WMS rendered images are for display, not numeric analysis. MRLC download workflow may require email. |
| Water exclusion polygons | USGS [NHD access](https://www.usgs.gov/national-hydrography/access-national-hydrography-products) | Verified live [ArcGIS layer 12, large-scale waterbodies](https://hydro.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/nhd/MapServer/12); query by study bounds, fetch IDs then batched geometry | Subtract lake/reservoir polygons and add river-area polygons where supplied. NHD was retired in 2023 and is not maintained; use newer 3DHP where available. Waterbody layer alone does not cover every river, coast or seasonal water feature. |
| Building exclusion polygons | FEMA / Oak Ridge National Laboratory [USA Structures](https://gis-fema.hub.arcgis.com/pages/usa-structures) | ArcGIS feature layers/downloads through [dataset page](https://gis-fema.hub.arcgis.com/datasets/fedmaps%3A%3Ausa-structures/about) | Image-derived building footprints; subtract mapped footprints. Catalog describes structures greater than 450 square feet, so small structures may be absent. Verify selected regional asset vintage and license. This is not parcel ownership or underground-utility data. |
| Conservation review | GreenInfo Network [CPAD / CCED](https://calands.org/get-data/), also [California state catalog](https://data.ca.gov/%40california-natural-resources-agency/california-protected-areas-database) | California protected lands and conservation easements; download form currently requests name/email | Use as management/conservation review flags. Do not exclude every park automatically or infer planting permission from public ownership. Preserve category, managing agency and restrictions when available; inspect release-specific terms. |

## Integration order

1. Download a pinned canopy product and matching-year land cover clipped to the existing study boundaries; compute area-weighted canopy estimates and valid-data coverage per screening polygon.
2. Add NHD water and FEMA building polygons as exclusion geometry. Record excluded area separately and mark all remaining area as unverified, not plantable.
3. Add a QA-screened multi-scene summer Landsat surface-temperature composite with dates, units, valid coverage and method. Never substitute a single scene for climatology or label modeled retrievals as observed air temperatures.
4. Add CPAD/CCED review flags. Utilities, parcel permissions, setbacks and habitat compatibility still require local verification.

Federal sources are publicly distributed; retain each asset's metadata/credits and verify release-specific terms. CPAD/CCED has separate distribution terms. Documentation and service metadata were inspected; full raster downloads and footprint coverage checks were not performed. The previously discovered ArcGIS `Hot_Spots` service belongs to Massachusetts (MAPC, EPSG:26986), so it is explicitly rejected for California.
